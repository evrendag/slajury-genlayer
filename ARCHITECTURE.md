# Architecture
`Claim` binds provider, service, tier, region, billing month, official incident, customer monitoring and claimed interval. Validators classify scope, customer impact, exclusion, reason and evidence-backed minutes.

Exact-consensus fields: scope, impact, exclusion, reason and evidence grade. Durations differ by at most five minutes and must map to the same deterministic credit band. The contract calculates month length, uptime and credit.

Security: HTTPS-only public sources, bounded intervals, untrusted-evidence prompt boundary, closed enums, impossible-field rejection and single finalization.
